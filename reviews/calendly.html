How it helps the realm

Nothing burns a realm's goodwill faster than scheduling friction. This ends the back-and-forth before it starts.

Boons

  • Once set up, booking a meeting takes the other party thirty seconds, no scroll exchange required.
  • Integrates cleanly with most calendars so double-bookings become rare.
  • Reliable and simple enough that neither party needs training to use it.

Curses

  • It handles scheduling and only scheduling; don't expect it to also manage the actual meeting.
  • Advanced routing and team scheduling features take some configuration to get right.

Common questions

Does the other person need an account to book time with me?

No, they just click a link and pick an open slot.

Can it handle team scheduling, not just one person's calendar?

Yes, team and round-robin scheduling are supported for larger setups.

Will it prevent double-booking across multiple calendars?

Yes, it checks connected calendars in real time before offering a slot.
