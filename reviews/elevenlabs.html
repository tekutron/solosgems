Pricing breakdown

  • Free: $0; 29 languages, a selection of pre-made voices, up to 3 custom voice clones, watermarked audio, no commercial use
  • Starter: $5–6/month; 30,000 characters/month, commercial rights, enough for a few short videos or podcast intros
  • Creator: $22/month; professional voice cloning, dubbing studio, higher character allowance
  • Pro: $99/month; 500,000 credits/month, expanded API access for automated workflows
  • Scale: $299/month; Business: $1,320/month or $13,200/year; Enterprise: custom

Annual billing saves roughly 17% (about two months free) across paid tiers.

What the plans don't include

Your LLM, telephony provider (if building a voice agent), and any usage beyond your plan's included character/credit limit are all billed separately on top of the subscription. If you're evaluating ElevenLabs for something beyond simple narration, like a voice-based customer interaction, budget for those add-ons before committing to a tier.

Pros

  • Voice quality is genuinely close to human, not obviously synthetic to most listeners
  • Starter tier at $5-6/month is a low-risk way to get commercial rights
  • Voice cloning lets a solopreneur maintain a consistent "voice" across content without re-recording

Cons

  • Free tier's no-commercial-use restriction makes it a demo, not a real trial for business use
  • Character/credit limits on lower tiers get eaten quickly by longer-form content
  • Jump from Creator ($22/mo) to Pro ($99/mo) is steep if you outgrow Creator's limits
