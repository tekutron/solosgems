Pricing breakdown

  • Free: basic invoicing, proposals, contracts, and time tracking
  • Basic: $9/user/month; track time and manage occasional projects
  • Essentials: $19/user/month; end-to-end client and project management, adds invoicing, contracts, proposals, and scheduling
  • Premium: $29/user/month; advanced client and project management with reporting, client portals, Gantt charts, QuickBooks and Zapier integration
  • Elite: $49/user/month; team management and reporting features with onboarding

Where the value actually is

Essentials covers what most solo freelancers need in one place: contracts with e-signatures, proposals, invoicing, and scheduling. Premium adds client portals, Gantt charts, and accounting integrations that matter more once you're running multiple concurrent projects or managing a subcontractor, features a true solo operator usually doesn't need yet. Start on Essentials and only move up once a specific Premium feature is something you're missing on a weekly basis, not a nice-to-have.

Pros

  • Free plan exists so you can test the core invoicing and contract flow before paying
  • Essentials tier covers what most solo freelancers actually need in one place
  • Client portal and e-signature on contracts look professional to clients

Cons

  • Jumping to Premium just for client portals or QuickBooks sync is a steep step up from Essentials
  • Project management features are thinner than dedicated tools like ClickUp
  • Per-user pricing means it gets expensive fast if you ever bring on a subcontractor

Common questions

Which Bonsai plan should a freelancer actually get?

Essentials at $19/month covers contracts, invoicing, and the core client-management workflow most freelancers need. Premium's client portals and Gantt charts are aimed at bigger, more complex projects, skip it until you're actually managing work at that scale.

Does Bonsai replace a dedicated project management tool?

Not fully. Its project management features are thinner than dedicated tools like ClickUp, Bonsai's strength is the contracts-and-invoicing side of freelance operations, not deep task or timeline management.

What happens to the price if I bring on a subcontractor?

It jumps. Bonsai bills per user, so adding even one subcontractor or collaborator to your workspace increases the monthly cost, worth factoring in before you plan to scale past solo.
