Typeform presents one question at a time in a full-screen, conversational format rather than a dense list of fields all at once, a design choice that consistently raises completion rates on longer forms compared to a traditional form layout, which is the core reason businesses pay for it over free alternatives.

Typeform's AI layer now generates form questions from a plain-language prompt and can summarize response data automatically, reducing the manual work of both building a form and analyzing what came back from it.

Example from typeform.com. Not independently tested by Solos Gems.

Pricing breakdown

  • Basic (from $28/mo): Unlimited typeforms, custom domain, remove Typeform branding, basic integrations
  • Plus (from $56/mo): Higher response and question limits, drop-off analysis, advanced logic
  • Business (from $91/mo): Priority support, higher usage caps, advanced reporting
  • Free: Limited responses per month to test the conversational format

Is it worth it over a free form tool

The honest comparison is against Google Forms or a free tier of a competing form builder, both of which cost nothing and handle basic data collection fine. Typeform's premium is specifically for completion rate: if a form is a client intake step, a paid application, or anything where a visitor abandoning halfway through costs you real business, the conversational format's measurably higher completion rate can justify the monthly cost. For low-stakes internal surveys, it's hard to justify over a free alternative.

Pros

  • Conversational, one-question format measurably improves completion rates on longer forms
  • AI-assisted question generation speeds up building a new form from scratch
  • Response summarization cuts down manual analysis of open-ended answers

Cons

  • Priced well above free alternatives like Google Forms for the same basic function
  • Entry Basic tier at $28/month still caps responses and features
  • Not clearly worth it unless form completion rate is tied to actual revenue
