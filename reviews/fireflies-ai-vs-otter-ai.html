Fireflies.ai vs. Otter.ai

Compared August 3, 2026 · Pricing and scores pulled from our own verified reviews of each tool

Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai both auto-transcribe and summarize meetings. Fireflies (#3, 8.4/10) wins on value with unlimited transcription minutes even on its free tier; Otter (#6, 8.0/10) is priced from $8.33-$16.99/month on Pro and caps its free plan at 300 minutes. For a solo operator with a heavy meeting load, Fireflies' free tier is the more durable starting point.
