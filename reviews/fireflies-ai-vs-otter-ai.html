Fireflies.ai, in short

Sitting silently in every meeting so nobody on the party has to scribble notes while also trying to talk. Meetings are where good quests get decided and forgotten in the same breath. A permanent scribe means the decisions actually survive until next week's council.

Otter.ai, in short

Transcribing meetings and letting you ask the transcript follow-up questions afterward, like an oracle who was actually paying attention. Nobody remembers the third item on a five-item agenda a week later. A searchable transcript means the realm's decisions don't evaporate.

Who should pick which

If meeting volume is genuinely high and you want a scribe that never runs out of stamina, Fireflies' unlimited transcription minutes make it the more durable default for a solo operator who lives on calls. If you're already comfortable in Otter's interface or specifically prefer its polish, it remains a fair choice, just steer clear of its team-sized tier built for a party of five when you're a party of one.

Common questions

Which has no cap on transcription minutes?

Fireflies. Its unlimited transcription minutes apply even on the free tier, unlike Otter's monthly cap.

Does Otter have any real advantage?

Mainly interface preference, or if you already use it elsewhere and would rather not switch tools mid-campaign.

Is either one better for searching past meetings?

Both offer searchable transcripts and AI summaries; the meaningful difference for a solo operator is really the transcription minute cap, not the search feature itself.
