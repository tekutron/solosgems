Boons

  • Built specifically for solo operators, not scaled-down enterprise software.
  • Automatic categorization of income and expenses saves real bookkeeping time.
  • Simple enough that no accounting background is required to get started.

Curses

  • It's intentionally basic; a growing business will outgrow it and need proper accounting software.
  • Some more advanced tax and reporting features are limited compared to full QuickBooks.

Common questions

Is this enough if my business grows beyond just me?

Likely not for long; it's intentionally scoped to solo operators and will feel limited as you grow.

Do I need bookkeeping experience to use it?

No, it's designed to be approachable without accounting background.

Does it handle invoicing too, or just expense tracking?

Yes, basic invoicing is included alongside expense and income tracking.
