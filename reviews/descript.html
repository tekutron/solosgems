Boons

  • Editing audio by deleting text is genuinely faster than dragging waveforms around for hours.
  • The overdub feature can patch a flubbed line without a re-recording session.
  • Filler-word removal happens almost automatically once you know where the button lives.

Curses

  • The sheer number of features means a real learning curve before you're fast with it.
  • Rendering longer projects can take a while, so budget for it rather than expecting instant turnaround.

Common questions

Do I need audio-editing experience to use it?

Not really. If you can edit a document, the text-based editing model will feel familiar quickly.

Can it fix a single flubbed word without a full re-record?

Yes, the overdub feature can patch individual words convincingly.

Is it built for video too, or just audio?

Both. It handles podcast audio and video editing in the same transcript-driven workflow.
