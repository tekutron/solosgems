Boons

  • Genuinely impressive for turning a described idea into a working prototype fast.
  • No coding knowledge required to get started.
  • Good for validating an idea before investing in a full custom build.

Curses

  • Costs for meaningful, ongoing app usage add up quickly compared to a from-scratch build.
  • Complex, highly custom functionality eventually hits the limits of the automated approach.

Common questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No, that's the core appeal, though understanding code helps for advanced customization.

Is the output production-ready, or just a prototype?

It can go further than a rough prototype, but complex production needs may need real development eventually.

Can I export the code and take it elsewhere?

Often yes, depending on the plan, giving some flexibility to move off the platform later.
