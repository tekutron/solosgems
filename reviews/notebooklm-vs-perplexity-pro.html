Who should pick which

If your research work is mostly digesting your own documents, client notes, transcripts, past reports, NotebookLM's whole design is built around exactly that and answers nothing it can't trace back to a page you gave it. If you need a fast, cited answer pulled from the open realm rather than your own vault, Perplexity fills that different gap, though many travelers already carry a browsing-enabled oracle that covers the same ground. The two rarely compete for the same question.

Common questions

Which is better for research grounded in my own documents?

NotebookLM, clearly. It's built specifically to answer from your own uploaded sources and deliberately won't answer outside them.

Do I need a second oracle if I already have one with web browsing?

Probably not. Perplexity's edge is citation quality on open-web research, but it overlaps heavily with browsing-enabled assistants many people already use.

Can both be used for different tasks?

Yes, and it's a reasonable split: NotebookLM for anything grounded in your own documents, Perplexity for open-web research, they aren't really competing for the same job.
