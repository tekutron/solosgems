Apollo.io combines a large contact database with AI-assisted outreach sequencing, letting you find prospects matching specific criteria, company size, industry, role, and then build and send automated, personalized outreach sequences without manually researching and emailing each contact one by one.

Apollo.io's differentiator is combining lead database access and outreach automation in one platform, rather than needing a separate contact database tool and a separate email sequencing tool, which matters for a solopreneur who can't justify paying for two separate sales tools.

Example from apollo.io. Not independently tested by Solos Gems.

Pricing breakdown

  • Free: Limited email credits and contact access to test the platform
  • Basic ($49/user/mo billed annually, ~$59/mo monthly): Unlimited email credits (minimum 120,000/year), core prospecting tools
  • Professional ($79/user/mo billed annually, ~$99/mo monthly): Adds US-only Auto Dialer, advanced reports, email tracking, higher credit limits
  • Organization: Custom pricing for larger sales teams

Why the real cost runs higher than advertised

Apollo's advertised per-user price looks reasonable next to dedicated sales tools, but the credit-based system for contact reveals and enrichment means actual monthly cost often runs 2-3x the sticker price once you're actively prospecting at volume, not just holding a seat. Before committing, run the math on your actual expected outreach volume, contacts revealed, sequences sent, against the credit allowances on each tier, rather than budgeting from the base subscription price alone.

Pros

  • Combines lead database and outreach automation in one platform, no need for two separate tools
  • Basic tier's unlimited email credits cover high-volume cold outreach
  • Professional tier's Auto Dialer adds phone outreach without a separate tool

Cons

  • Real costs often run 2-3x the advertised price due to credit-based overages
  • Steeper setup and learning curve than a simple email tool
  • Narrow relevance, no value if your business doesn't do direct sales prospecting
