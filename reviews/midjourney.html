Pricing breakdown

  • Basic: $10/month, or $96/year (~$8/month); about 3.3 fast GPU hours/month
  • Standard: $30/month, or $288/year (~$24/month); about 15 fast GPU hours/month, plus unlimited Relax Mode (slower generation)
  • Pro: $60/month, or $576/year (~$48/month); about 30 fast GPU hours/month, stronger privacy options
  • Mega: $120/month, or $1,152/year (~$96/month); about 60 fast GPU hours/month

There's no free plan, the only free trial option is a limited one through the Niji Journey mobile app.

Why Standard beats Basic for most solo use

Standard and higher plans include Relax Mode: unlimited image generation at slower speed once you've used your fast GPU hours. Basic doesn't include Relax Mode, so once its 3.3 fast hours run out mid-month, you're stuck waiting for the next billing cycle or buying more. For a solopreneur who isn't generating images under a same-day deadline, Standard's unlimited (if slower) generation is worth the extra $20/month over Basic.

Common questions

Can I try Midjourney for free before paying?

No, there's no free plan, which means no real way to test fit before committing to a subscription. Budget for at least the Basic tier to properly evaluate whether it fits your workflow.

Is the cheapest Basic plan enough to use regularly?

Not really. Basic's 3.3 GPU hours run out fast if you're iterating on prompts, which most first-time users do, Standard is the more realistic entry point for regular use.

Can I use Midjourney images in paid client work?

Confirm current terms before assuming you can. Commercial usage rights and content ownership rules have changed over time, verify the current policy directly rather than relying on older information before using output in paid work.
