Canva Magic Studio vs. Photoroom

Compared August 3, 2026 · Pricing and scores pulled from our own verified reviews of each tool

Canva Magic Studio (#1, 8.7/10) and Photoroom (#7, 7.9/10) both use AI for visual content, but they're built for different jobs. Canva is a general design tool, background removal, resizing, on-brand generation, priced around $10-15/month for Pro. Photoroom is purpose-built specifically for product photography and e-commerce listings, starting at $7.50/month billed yearly. If you're selling physical products, Photoroom's specialization tends to produce cleaner results faster; for general marketing graphics, Canva's breadth wins.

Verdict
Photoroom if product photos for an online store or reselling account are the core need. Canva Magic Studio for everything else, social graphics, presentations, general on-brand design work.

Canva Magic Studio does a bit of everything reasonably well. Photoroom does one thing, background removal, extremely well. If your problem is specifically my product photos have my kitchen counter in them, Photoroom wins that fight.

Canva Magic Studio, in short

Canva Magic Studio bundles AI design tools, background removal, resizing, on-brand generation, into Canva Pro, and is the top-ranked tool on this site. It's worth it the moment you need more than three graphics a month, though AI credit usage runs out faster than the pricing page implies.

Who should pick which

If your AI design needs span social posts, presentations, and general marketing graphics, Canva Magic Studio's breadth justifies the ~$10-15/month cost. If your specific, recurring need is clean product photos for an online store, reselling account, or print-on-demand shop, Photoroom's purpose-built tooling and lower price point make it the more direct fit, and the two aren't mutually exclusive if budget allows both.

Common questions

Is Canva Magic Studio or Photoroom better for a solopreneur?

Canva Magic Studio is the better general-purpose design tool and is Solos Gems' top-ranked tool overall. Photoroom is the better choice specifically for product photography and e-commerce listings, and costs less starting at $7.50/month. Solopreneurs selling physical products often get more direct value from Photoroom; everyone else is better served by Canva's broader toolset.

Do I need both Canva Magic Studio and Photoroom?

Only if you do both general design work and product photography. Photoroom is purpose-built for product photos, background removal, staging; Canva Magic Studio covers broader design needs, social graphics, presentations, on-brand marketing materials.

Which is cheaper for a solo seller?

Photoroom has a usable free tier and starts around $7.50/month, generally the cheaper entry point if product photography is your only need. Canva Magic Studio runs $10-15/month and covers a wider range of design tasks.

Which should I pick if I only sell products online?

Photoroom. It's purpose-built for exactly that use case, background removal and product staging, and outperforms a general design tool for that specific job.
