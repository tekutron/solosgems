Beehiiv positions itself as the newsletter platform built for monetization from day one, not an email tool that bolted on paid subscriptions later. For a content-driven solo site, that matters if the plan is to eventually charge for premium content or sell sponsorships.

Beehiiv is one of the only newsletter platforms with a built-in ad marketplace and payout tracking baked into the same dashboard that shows your open rate, most competitors require bolting on a separate sponsorship platform to monetize a list.

Example from beehiiv.com. Not independently tested by Solos Gems.

Pricing breakdown

  • Launch (Free): $0; up to 2,500 subscribers, unlimited email sends
  • Scale: from $43–49/month billed annually; adds monetization tools, surveys, A/B testing, and AI features
  • Max: from $96–109/month billed annually; removes Beehiiv branding, adds priority support and their NewsletterXP course
  • Enterprise: custom, above 100,000 subscribers

Both Scale and Max scale in price with your subscriber count, a newsletter with 50,000 subscribers pays roughly 5x the sticker price shown above, so the entry price isn't what you'll pay once you've grown.

The monetization math

Beehiiv charges 0% platform commission on paid subscription revenue across all plans, you only pay Stripe's standard 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. Compared to platforms that take a revenue cut on top of payment processing, that's a meaningfully better deal once a newsletter starts generating real subscription income.

Pros

  • Free tier supports up to 2,500 subscribers, enough to validate an audience before paying
  • 0% commission on paid subscriptions keeps more revenue for the creator
  • Built-in growth tools (referral programs, recommendations network) not bolted on as an afterthought

Cons

  • Pricing scales with subscriber count, so costs aren't predictable long-term
  • AI features are gated behind the Scale tier, not available free
  • Removing Beehiiv branding requires the more expensive Max tier

Common questions

Is Beehiiv's free plan enough to launch a newsletter?

Yes, the free tier covers up to 2,500 subscribers with no feature gate on the core publishing and monetization tools, generous enough to actually launch, grow, and start monetizing before paying anything.

Will Beehiiv get expensive as my list grows?

Plan for costs to climb with your subscriber count, not stay flat. Beehiiv's pricing scales by subscribers past the free tier, so a newsletter that grows successfully will cost more over time, budget accordingly rather than assuming a fixed monthly fee.

Can I remove Beehiiv's branding on a cheap plan?

No. Removing the platform's branding requires the Max tier, and the AI writing features are gated behind Scale, so a fully white-labeled, AI-assisted setup costs meaningfully more than the entry paid tier.
