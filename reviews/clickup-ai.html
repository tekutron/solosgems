ClickUp is a project management platform (tasks, docs, goals, time tracking) with an AI layer called ClickUp Brain sold as an add-on rather than bundled into the base price.

Brain's "Context Engine" (shown above) pulls answers from tasks, docs, and past decisions already sitting in your ClickUp workspace, rather than starting from a blank chat window with no memory of your projects.

Example from clickup.com/brain. Not independently tested by Solos Gems.

Base plan pricing

  • Free: $0 forever, unlimited members, but constrained on storage and features
  • Unlimited: $7/user/month billed annually ($10/month monthly)
  • Business: $12/user/month billed annually ($19/month monthly)
  • Enterprise: custom pricing

ClickUp Brain add-on pricing

Brain AI now comes in two tiers stacked on top of a paid base plan (published figures vary slightly by source: confirm the current rate before budgeting, but expect roughly this range):

  • Brain AI: around $7–9/user/month; unlimited AI assistant queries, AI chat, premium model access, writing assistance, and a monthly pool of AI Super Credits
  • Everything AI: around $28/user/month; adds the agentic suite, an AI notetaker, and unlimited AI fields/automations

Heavier AI features draw down a shared credit pool priced around $10 per 10,000 credits on top of the seat fee.

Why the "seat tax" complaint doesn't apply solo

Brain is billed on every paid member in a workspace, not per actual AI user, a real problem for a 30-person team where only 5 people use it, since they'd pay for all 30 seats. As a solo operator, that structural complaint doesn't apply: you have exactly one seat, so you're only ever paying for the AI you actually use.

Pros

  • Solo pricing avoids the "seat tax" problem that hurts larger teams
  • Free base plan lets you evaluate the project management side before paying for AI at all
  • AI features live inside the same workspace as your tasks, not a separate tool to context-switch into

Cons

  • Total cost (base plan + Brain add-on) adds up faster than a single flat subscription
  • Published add-on pricing has been inconsistent across sources recently: verify current cost before subscribing
  • Everything AI's $28/user/month is a steep jump if you want the full agentic feature set

Common questions

Is ClickUp Brain worth adding if I already use ClickUp?

The per-seat AI math actually favors solo users here, you're not paying for unused seats the way a larger team would with a flat AI add-on. If you're already on ClickUp for tasks, the incremental $7-9/month is a reasonable add rather than a new subscription.

How much does ClickUp Brain actually cost total?

More than just the add-on price. Total cost is the base ClickUp plan ($7-10/month) plus the Brain add-on ($7-9/month), so budget for both, not just the AI line item, when comparing it to a single flat-fee tool.

Should I verify the current price before subscribing?

Yes. Published add-on pricing has been inconsistent across sources recently, confirm the current cost directly on ClickUp's pricing page before budgeting, rather than relying on older published figures.
