Pricing runs from a free tier (50 message credits/month) to Hobby at $32/month, Standard at $120/month, and Pro at $400/month, all billed yearly. The site's verdict is the Hobby tier is a reasonable test for light support volume, but Standard's $120/month is a real ongoing cost that only makes sense once support inquiries are eating meaningful time.

Chatbase works by ingesting your existing content, a help center, product docs, your website, then answering visitor questions grounded in that material rather than a generic AI chatbot with no context on your specific business, product, or policies.

Chatbase's differentiator is training the chatbot exclusively on your own content, so answers stay consistent with your actual policies and product details instead of a general-purpose assistant improvising plausible-sounding but incorrect answers about your business.

Pricing breakdown

  • Free: 50 message credits/month, 1 member, limited access to models
  • Hobby ($32/mo billed yearly, $384/yr): 700 message credits/month, access to advanced models
  • Standard ($120/mo billed yearly, $1,440/yr): 4,000 message credits/month, 3 members
  • Pro ($400/mo billed yearly, $4,800/yr): 15,000 message credits/month, 5 members
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing with higher limits and flexible billing

Is a chatbot worth it before you have support volume

For a genuinely small solo business, the free or Hobby tier is enough to test whether an AI chatbot actually reduces your support load, before committing to Standard's $120/month. The math only works once you're spending real hours a week answering the same handful of questions repeatedly; if your support volume is a few emails a week, a well-organized FAQ page may do the same job at no cost, and the chatbot becomes worth paying for only once volume and repetition justify it.

Common questions

Can I run Chatbase for free?

There's a free Hobby tier that's a fair test for light support volume, a handful of conversations a month. It's a real trial, not just a demo, but you'll outgrow it once support volume becomes real and steady.

Is the jump to Standard worth $120/month?

Only once your support load is real and measurable. Standard's price is a genuine jump from Hobby's $32/month, worth paying once the chatbot is handling a volume of questions that would otherwise eat real support time.

Does Chatbase work well without good source content?

No. It needs decent material to train on, help docs, FAQs, product pages. Garbage in produces garbage answers, so budget time to prepare good source content before expecting useful results.
