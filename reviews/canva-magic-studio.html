Common questions

Do I need any design training to use this?

No. If you can drag a rectangle and pick a color, you can finish a usable design in one sitting.

Will it work for print materials, not just social posts?

Yes, it handles print-ready exports, though a professional printer's guild may still want their own proofing pass.

Can a whole party collaborate on one design?

Yes, multiple people can edit the same banner at once, which is either efficient teamwork or chaos, depending on your party.
