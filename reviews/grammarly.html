Boons

  • Works everywhere you type, from emails to guild forums, not just one walled document.
  • Tone suggestions catch when a message reads harsher than intended before you send it to an ally.
  • Genuinely useful free tier catches the basics without requiring a coin up front.

Curses

  • It has strong opinions about your writing voice and doesn't always know when 'wrong' is actually 'stylistic'.
  • Constant suggestions can turn into constant interruptions if you let it hover over everything.

Common questions

Does it work outside of one specific app?

Yes, it runs as a browser extension and desktop app across most places you write.

Can it help with tone, not just spelling?

Yes, tone detection is one of its more useful features for messages to clients or allies.

Will it rewrite my voice entirely?

It can, if you let it. Accepting every suggestion tends to flatten a distinct voice into a generic one.
