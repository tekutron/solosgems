How it helps the realm

A realm this sprawling needs a scribe who actually reads the footnotes. This is the one oracle that shows its sources instead of just declaring things true.

Boons

  • Answers are grounded in the documents you gave it, not a wizard's vague recollection of the internet.
  • Turns a pile of meeting notes into an audio briefing you can listen to during the commute back to the keep.
  • The free tier covers most solo adventurers without ever needing to unlock a paid wing.

Curses

  • It only knows what you feed it. Ask about anything outside your uploaded scrolls and it goes quiet.
  • Uploading a truly massive archive takes patience the impatient adventurer may not have.

Common questions

Can it work with my own files instead of the open internet?

Yes, that is the entire point. You upload your sources and it answers strictly from those.

Is it good for turning notes into something I can listen to?

Yes, the audio-briefing feature is one of its best tricks for reviewing material hands-free.

Does it cite where its answers come from?

Yes, every answer links back to the specific passage in your uploaded material.
