Pricing breakdown

  • Free: 400 AI credits, granted once at signup, do not refresh; enough for roughly 10 full presentations depending on complexity
  • Plus: $8/month billed annually ($10/month monthly)
  • Pro: $15/month billed annually ($20/month monthly); 4,000 monthly credits
  • Ultra: around $90/month; 20,000 monthly credits

The credit math isn't fixed

Credit cost varies by the model and task involved, so a stated credit allowance isn't a guaranteed number of finished decks, a text-heavy deck costs less than one full of AI-generated images. If you're testing on the free plan, know that those 400 credits are a one-time grant, not a monthly reset, which changes how you should budget your first month of use.

Common questions

Do Gamma's free credits refresh monthly?

No, and that's a real trap if you're evaluating it over a few months. Free credits are a one-time allowance, not a monthly refresh, so don't build an ongoing workflow around the free tier expecting it to renew.

What burns through Gamma's credits fastest?

Heavy image generation. Text-heavy decks and one-pagers are relatively credit-light, but leaning on AI-generated imagery inside a deck burns credits fast and somewhat unpredictably, worth watching if you're on a lower tier.

Is Gamma a substitute for a real slide deck tool?

For speed, yes, for deep customization, not quite. Design customization is more limited than building from scratch in PowerPoint or Keynote, Gamma's strength is getting a decent-looking pitch or one-pager out fast, not pixel-level control.
