Synthesia generates video of an AI avatar speaking your script in one of over 100 available avatar options, letting you produce training material, product explainers, or onboarding content without appearing on camera yourself or hiring a presenter and production crew.

Synthesia's differentiator is the breadth and quality of its avatar library combined with an AI script assistant, letting you go from a written script to a polished, presenter-led video without any camera equipment, filming, or editing skill required.

Example from synthesia.io. Not independently tested by Solos Gems.

Pricing breakdown

  • Starter (from $14/mo): 125+ avatars, AI script assistant, up to 10 video minutes/month
  • Creator (from $59/mo): 180+ avatars, custom fonts, audio downloads, higher monthly minute allowance
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for teams needing custom avatars and higher volume

The minute cap is the real constraint

Synthesia's pricing is built around monthly video minutes rather than a flat unlimited allowance, and even the entry Starter tier's 10 minutes a month is genuinely limiting if you're producing regular training or onboarding content. This tool fits best as an occasional-use solution, a handful of explainer or training videos a month, rather than a primary content production pipeline, where the minute economics would push costs up fast on higher tiers.

Pros

  • Avatar quality is genuinely convincing for training and explainer-style content
  • AI script assistant speeds up going from an outline to a finished script
  • No camera, filming, or on-camera talent required at all

Cons

  • Monthly minute caps are limiting even on paid tiers
  • Narrow use case, not a general video creation or editing tool
  • Avatar-led video style doesn't fit every brand or content type
