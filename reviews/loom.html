Best used for

Recording a quick screen-and-voice video to explain something instead of writing three paragraphs nobody will fully read.

How it helps the realm

A quick recorded explanation travels faster than a written scroll and gets read (watched) more often. This makes recording one nearly effortless.

Boons

  • Recording and sharing a video takes about the same effort as writing an email.
  • Viewers can leave timestamped comments directly on the video for async back-and-forth.
  • AI-generated summaries and chapters make long recordings easier to skim later.

Curses

  • Video takes longer to make and consume than a well-written short note for simple messages.
  • Some of the AI editing features sit behind a higher tier than the free plan offers.

Common questions

Is this better than just writing an email for quick updates?

For anything visual or process-based, usually yes. For a one-line update, writing is still faster.

Can viewers respond without recording their own video?

Yes, text comments and reactions are supported alongside video replies.

Does it work for both quick updates and longer tutorials?

Yes, it handles both, though very long recordings benefit from Descript-style editing tools instead.
