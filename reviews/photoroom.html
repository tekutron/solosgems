Boons

  • Background removal is near-instant and shockingly clean, even around fiddly edges like hair or fur.
  • Batch processing means outfitting an entire market stall's worth of product photos doesn't take all day.
  • Mobile app makes it easy to shoot and clean up a photo on the spot.

Curses

  • It's built specifically for product and portrait photos; ask it to do general illustration work and it shrugs.
  • Some of the fancier background-generation features sit behind a higher tier.

Yes, the mobile app is genuinely capable, not a stripped-down afterthought.
