News mention volume comes from GDELT's free public API, a 3-day rolling window of global news coverage. Search interest comes from the Wikimedia Pageviews API. Build activity comes from GitHub's public search API, filtered to repos tagged "ai" created in the last 30 days. None of this is simulated, all of it can shift as the underlying data does, and low or missing numbers for a region reflect the data source, not a judgment about that region.
