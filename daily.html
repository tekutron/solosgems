          How to play the Herald's Riddle
          
    
          Each day the Herald hides one AI tool from the Solos Gems database behind its own comic, blurred past recognition. Type a guess and pick a tool from the list. Any of the tools in the database counts as a guess, but only one is today's answer.
          
    
          Reading the signs
          
    
          Every guess comes back with three runes, compared against the hidden tool:
          
    
            
      
          • Guild: green if your guess is in the same category, such as Voice and Music or Coding and Dev Tools.
            • 
      
          • Coin: green if it has the same pricing, a permanent free plan or paid only.
            • 
      
          • Rank: an arrow pointing toward the answer on the Herald's ranked list. Up means the answer ranks higher (a smaller number), down means lower.
            • 
    
          
    
          The clues, in order
          
    
          You start with the tool's category. Each miss (or skip) reveals the next clue: its pricing, its rank and score, the comic's caption, a cool fact with the name scrubbed out, and finally the shape of its name. The fog over the comic thins with every turn, so by your last guess you are nearly looking at it directly.
          
    
          Streaks, sharing, and practice
          
    
          Your record and streak are kept in your own browser, nothing is sent anywhere. When you finish, share your result as a little grid of runes that shows how you did without spoiling the answer. Want more? The Practice dungeon serves random riddles from the whole database and never touches your streak.
          
    
          Every answer is a real tool, with a real comic, rank, and plain-English summary in the AI tools database. Prefer stories to riddles? Try The Road to Solos Gems.
