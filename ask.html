Who is the Herald?

The Herald is a general-purpose AI assistant, like the big chatbots, with one difference: it talks like Solos Gems. Ask how to boil an egg and you get the right timings, plus the observation that peeling it is the real boss fight. The answer always comes first. The humor is seasoning.

How it works

Under the cloak is Meta's open Llama 3.1 model with 8 billion parameters, running on Cloudflare. The Herald's voice comes from Solos Gems writing: a style guide and example answers drawn from a dataset we wrote ourselves, with hundreds of everyday questions answered correctly in the Herald's style and a few hundred original jokes that describe the real world as if it were a fantasy realm (a free trial is an innkeeper whose key starts billing on day eight). The style shapes how the Herald talks. Its knowledge comes from the underlying model, not from our tool rankings.

What it's good at

  • Explanations: science, history, money, technology, "explain it like I'm five".
  • Everyday help: cooking, cleaning, fixing things, planning, studying.
  • Writing: emails, messages, essays, poems, names for your D&D character.
  • Code: short examples and explanations in common languages.

What to keep in mind

The Herald is a smaller model than the giants in the castle towers, and like every AI it can be confidently wrong. It can't browse the web or see today's news, and it isn't a doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor. Double-check anything important, and in an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Privacy

Your conversation is sent to the Solos Gems server and processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to write the reply. We don't store your conversations or link them to you, and the chat clears when you leave the page. Please don't share personal details like passwords, card numbers, or health records.
